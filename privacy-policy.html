Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 07/09/2026

1. Overview

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We care about your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our site, how we use it, and how your data is handled via third-party services and your browser storage.

2. Information Collected Automatically (Analytics)

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our website.

What is tracked:

GA4 automatically tracks information such as your general location (city/country), browser type, device type, pages visited, and the duration of your visit.

IP Addresses:

GA4 anonymises IP addresses by default, meaning your full IP address is not stored by Google.

Purpose:

We use this data strictly to analyse traffic and improve user experience. We do not use this data for targeted advertising.

Cookie Deployment:

We do not currently display a cookie banner, and Google Analytics cookies are deployed automatically upon visiting the site. We intend to implement a cookie consent banner at a later date to allow users to opt-in or opt-out directly.

Opt-Out:

Until the consent banner is active, you can prevent Google Analytics from tracking your activity by disabling cookies in your browser settings or by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Because we use Google Analytics, cookies are used on this site to distinguish unique users and analyse performance. They do not contain personally identifiable information like your name or email address. You can choose to clear or decline these cookies at any time via your web browser settings.

4. Local Browser Storage (User Notes)

Our site includes a feature that allows you to write and save notes.

On-Device Storage:

Your notes are stored entirely within your own web browser using local storage (localStorage).

No Server Transfer:

This data remains strictly on your device. We do not upload, store, collect, or have any access to your notes on our servers.

How to Clear:

You can permanently delete your notes at any time by clearing your notes within the application or by wiping your browser's history and site data.

Third-Party Services and Hosting

GitHub Pages

Our site is hosted by GitHub, Inc. When you visit our website, GitHub may automatically collect standard technical log data (such as your IP address and web browser type) for security and operational purposes. For more details, please review the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Google Fonts

This website loads fonts externally using Google Fonts. When you load a page, your browser establishes a direct connection with Google's servers to download the fonts. Google may log request data such as your IP address and browser version for technical and analytical purposes.

6. Open Source and Transparency

We believe in full transparency regarding how your data is handled. The complete source code for this website is open source and available for review. You can inspect how our note-saving feature and analytics scripts are implemented by visiting our GitHub Repository.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: hello@filmhubmidlands.org
